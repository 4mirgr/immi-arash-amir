# English testing and unfinished specialist training

Official sources checked **2026-11-03**. This is a generic evidence register, not an individual eligibility determination. Booking policies and registration rules must be rechecked before paying or applying. No private CV facts are published here.

## OET, Pearson and restrictions: keep the issues separate

- **OET is not Pearson PTE.** OET's [official about page](https://oet.com/en-us/about) says it is owned by Cambridge Boxhill Language Assessment Unit Trust, a venture between Cambridge English (Aus) and Box Hill Institute. Full page retrieved.
- OET's published [sanctioned-country policy](https://support.oet.com/articles/Knowledge/What-are-sanctioned-countries) states: “We currently cannot offer testing or receive payments (funds) from candidates residing in sanctioned countries.” It lists **Iran**. Its compliance explanation names third-party delivery vendor **Prometric**. Full article retrieved through this alternate Salesforce URL; the `/s/article/` version returned only a loading/CSS-error shell.
- This is direct evidence of a **residence/payment/service restriction**, not proof of a universal ban based solely on Iranian nationality wherever the person lives. Do not infer that travelling briefly abroad automatically changes residence or eligibility. Written provider clarification for the candidate's actual residence, ID, test mode and payment arrangement is required before booking; do not misrepresent those facts.
- Searches found reports about Pearson/PTE restrictions, but this pass did **not establish an exact current official PTE Academic rule covering all Iranian nationals abroad**. [Pearson policy centre](https://www.pearsonpte.com/policy-center/), [terms](https://www.pearsonpte.com/policy-center/terms-and-conditions.html), [identification policy](https://www.pearsonpte.com/policy-center/identification-policy.html) and [user agreement](https://www.pearsonpte.com/policy-center/user-agreement.html) were read. The identification policy requires a valid passport for PTE Academic taken outside one's country of citizenship; that is an ID rule and does not independently prove permission to book despite sanctions.
- The official **PTE Express** policy surfaced in searches is a different product and must not be used as proof of PTE Academic eligibility. Advocacy reports are leads, not a substitute for the provider's current written policy. Keep any broader Pearson restriction claim marked **unverified**, rather than dismissing the reported concern or treating it as established for OET.

## IELTS availability is a separate booking issue

The full [IDP Iran announcement](https://ielts.idp.com/iran/about/ielts-announcement) says IDP decided to stop IELTS testing in Iran and the final test would be **31 January 2026**, citing financial and regulatory factors. It states existing results remain accepted and links free preparation resources.

Some official IDP centre/listing pages still surface Iranian centres and limited-space wording in search. **Those listings are not proof of a presently bookable seat.** Use the dated cessation announcement and an actual current booking confirmation from the provider. IELTS remains a preparation option, but budget/plan for a possible overseas test and confirm passport acceptance, entry requirements, available mode and payment before booking travel. This evidence does not assert a worldwide nationality ban or certify another partner's in-country availability.

## Ahpra / Medical Board English test pathway

Full [Ahpra accepted-tests page](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/Registration/Registration-Standards/English-language-skills/Accepted-English-language-tests) retrieved. It announces score updates effective **23 April 2026** and separates tests taken before and after that date. Use the table matching the actual test date; do not apply new OET/PTE thresholds retrospectively without the transition policy.

For a future **IELTS Academic** test, the current table requires:

| Requirement | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Overall | 7 |
| Listening | 7 |
| Reading | 7 |
| Speaking | 7 |
| Writing | 6.5 |

- Accepted IELTS formats are **Academic paper or computer at a test centre**. Do not substitute IELTS General Training, IELTS Online/at-home or an indicative practice result.
- Normally results must be within **two years before lodging the registration application**. The page describes limited exceptions tied to continuous English-language practice/study in specified settings; do not assume an exception applies.
- Results may come from one sitting or a maximum of two sittings within a 12-month period, under the full component/overall and floor-score conditions. Different providers cannot be combined. IELTS One Skill Retake is accepted subject to the stated rules, including the provider's 60-day eligibility window and applicable overall timing.
- A self-estimated CEFR level such as B2 is a planning baseline, **not an IELTS score, language certificate or proof of meeting the registration standard**. Start with a four-skill diagnostic and adjust preparation from measured performance.
- Registration, visa English requirements/points and a particular employer's requirements are separate decisions. Ahpra's Writing 6.5 threshold must not be advertised as sufficient for every visa or employer. Schedule the final test against the likely application date and expiry window instead of booking prematurely just because preparation starts now.

The [English language skills registration standard](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/Registration/Registration-Standards/English-language-skills/English-language-skills-registration-standard.aspx) was also retrieved; the current accepted-tests/transition guidance is the source for test-date-specific scores.

## Specialist training still in progress: preserve both board outcomes

Full [RACP Standard Specialist Assessment Pathway](https://www.racp.edu.au/overseas-trained-physicians/international-medical-graduates/standard-specialist-assessment-pathway) retrieved:

> “To be eligible for specialist assessment, you must hold a verified international specialist qualification (minimum 3 years of specialist training) in one or more of our specialties.”

RACP compares training, assessments, recent practice, consultant experience and CPD with the Australian specialty. Shorter overseas training may require significant independent consultant experience; assessment is case-specific. Substantial, partial and not-comparable outcomes are distinct. An invitation from a supervisor is not a qualification award or a comparability decision.

Maintain these as separate branches until evidence resolves them:

1. **Training unfinished:** actual completion date, final award and board result remain pending. Do not describe the doctor as having completed specialty training or obtained a board qualification.
2. **Training completed and board passed:** obtain the exact award, awarding body, primary-source verification, curriculum/rotations, FTE duration, licence/scope and post-training experience. Board passage alone does not guarantee RACP eligibility or comparability.
3. **Training completed but board not obtained:** establish what qualification/completion certificate is actually awarded and what specialist practice rights it grants. Obtain RACP confirmation about that precise award. Do not assume either automatic eligibility or permanent ineligibility solely from the label “without board.”

Keep the user's migration goal in both post-completion branches. Any alternative employment, non-training hospital post, AMC/standard pathway or accredited training/RPL route still needs its own registration and recruitment checks; it is not automatic entry to a nominated training year. See the existing [Australian IMG pathway evidence](australian-img-pathways.md).

## Evidence boundaries and follow-up inputs

Before individual booking/application decisions, confirm: actual current residence and passport; preferred test city/mode and provider permission; four-skill diagnostic; test date; registration and visa target; confirmed training completion date; exact final qualification; board result; current licence and independently documented consultant experience. Store pending facts as pending and request updates at those milestones rather than inventing dates or treating old CV expectations as completed achievements.
