# Arash discovery and source evidence audit — 2026-11-17

This review follows the existing GP/PESCI/Level 1 job-search goals. It repairs concrete extraction and queue defects without replacing the application structure, expanding a paid service or treating private spreadsheet leads as vacancies. Raw clinic URLs, uploaded documents, CVs and actual contact records remain outside Git.

## Reproduced defects and corrections

1. **Published email discarded after extraction.** An ordinary `<a href="mailto:…">Email our practice</a>` produced a valid email but its quote contained only the generic label. The source-proof gate correctly rejected that incomplete receipt, leaving an enquiry without a usable recipient. A `visible_link` receipt now retains the short visible label plus the exact decoded mailto destination as attribute evidence. Multiple explicitly published To addresses stay separate. Query parameters, CC/BCC/body/subject content, hidden links and injected malformed addresses are excluded. No address is guessed or derived from a name.

2. **Long pages erased useful clinical context.** The HTML reader flattened every block into one line. A long welcome paragraph and punctuation-free service/training paragraphs could merge into a statement longer than the research bound; every useful statement was then discarded. The reader now preserves visible block boundaries while retaining inline wording and the same 50,000-character limit. Hidden elements and navigation remain excluded from clinical evidence. Clinical paragraphs retain exact source URL, observation time and their own text.

3. **Canonical hostname spelling lost related pages.** A root page at `clinic.example` could publish Contact/Services links at `www.clinic.example`; strict hostname equality discarded them. Extraction and the public related-page queue now allow that exact optional `www` alias. They do not follow arbitrary clinic subdomains or other organisations. The reader recognises an explicitly approved `www` host's apex alias, still validates robots and every redirect, and deduplicates same-page or same-destination aliases. Public service/training pages can now enter the existing depth-one queue alongside contact/team pages. The maximum remains three children per parent; private bank research still selects at most two related pages.

4. **A crashed final attempt remained “reading” forever.** The third claim incremented attempts to three. If that invocation expired before storing a result, the expired lease no longer met `attempts < 3`, so it could never leave the reading state. Successful rows whose final refresh crashed had the same problem. Exhausted interrupted leases now consume an existing work slot, record a failed observation, release their lease and invalidate stale public evidence without making another HTTP request or extending the retry allowance. The first slot prioritises this cleanup; the second retains the normal Italy/fallback rotation. Active unexpired leases remain protected by the existing lease predicate.

5. **Preclaimed same-host sites could overlap the next invocation.** Both private bank rows are claimed before same-host processing is serialized. The previous three-minute lease was shorter than the combined allowed waits, permitting the next five-minute tick to reclaim a still-active second site. The lease now derives from the actual exported reader caps: each site permits six HTTP timeouts of at most ten seconds, at most five document crawl delays of thirty seconds, and an eight-second membership check. Two serial sites plus thirty seconds of storage headroom round up to eight minutes. This changes no per-run request/query allowance. Every release and completion still compares the exact lease value; a delayed completion cannot overwrite a newer claim and reports `lease_lost` rather than a false stored-success receipt.

6. **Linked service pages were read but their content never reached letter preparation.** The opportunity reader previously merged child contacts and named roles only. It now provides a separate top-level `clinicEvidence` array, with at most four exact statements per linked page and twelve total. Each statement has its own source URL/read time and the current parent page ID/read time. The root advertisement text is not overwritten with another page's words. Both observations must be within seven days, all content/queue/enrichment timestamps and statuses must match, the child must share the same exact clinic host (allowing only the ordinary optional `www` spelling), and both reads must lie inside the selected advertisement edition's observation window. Standalone history/population categories, patient narratives and instructions are excluded from this service-evidence corpus; no clinic identity is independently verified by it. A publisher quote still requires review of whether it describes the target clinic.

7. **Parent refreshes could leave children permanently bound to an old observation.** The old child insert ignored all existing URLs and selected zero candidates once the three-child cap was full. The existing single insert statement now refreshes currently published links owned by the same parent even at the cap, admits new URLs only within remaining capacity and never rebinds a shared URL belonging to another parent. Child queue and enrichment records carry the exact parent read time. If the parent changes while a child is in flight, the older result cannot acquire the newer relationship or erase its queued refresh request. The claim's parent generation is retained through final-attempt crash recovery too; a genuinely newer parent gets its own bounded reading cycle without reviving the exhausted older cycle. Legacy unbound child text is not silently accepted as current evidence.

## End-to-end result and bounds

A synthetic regression begins with a long homepage, a generic mailto label and a service page on the canonical hostname alias. Reading that clinic now produces a complete four-paragraph enquiry using its published email, the independently read service statement and the authenticated owner's matching CV passage. The resulting record still says lead-only, vacancy-not-verified, supervision-not-verified and sending-not-approved. The recipient, source quote, signature and fixed paragraphs do not require invented details or a manual clinic note.

The changes add no schema, cron, database query, model call or provider transport. The public reader still processes at most two work slots per invocation and six HTTP requests per page attempt. Private clinic observations retain their existing two-site/six-request caps and same-owner checks. Existing bank records receive the improvements on their ordinary fresh read or refresh; this is not an immediate reread of the entire bank or a new extraction-version rollout. Availability restrictions, robots, PDF/dynamic-page limitations and actual completion counters remain separate operational facts.

## Verification

The corrected focused set passes 180 tests covering the public reader/store/extractor, clinic bank/research/enquiry, canonical redirects, contact coverage, background budgets, opportunity views/mail and owner membership. The linked-evidence tests cover current source attribution with the same four read queries, rejected foreign/stale/unbound/mismatched receipts, protection of an older publication from later child content, full-cap parent refresh, an older in-flight response, crashed final child attempts, and shared-URL ownership.
