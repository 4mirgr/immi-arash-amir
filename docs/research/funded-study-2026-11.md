# Funded medical and health research study — source review, 2026-11-03

Scope: international applicants considering Australian or European research degrees. This is a source-grounded preparation catalogue, not a finding that a particular person is eligible, a live vacancy list or a clinical registration assessment. The corresponding public, machine-readable catalogue is `cloud/study-evidence.json`; it contains no CV, grades, email, passport or private application details. Only the authenticated Amir workspace should receive this study catalogue. Individual readiness must be derived server-side from that owner's private evidence.

Read method: full official page text retrieved using Exa on 2026-11-03, following targeted discovery. Reviewed material may be indexed rather than a live snapshot; dates, amounts, host rules and actual open status must be rechecked before submission. A catalogue review date does not mean an offer or funding award is active. Search discovery used eight queries of five results (40 returned results); duplicated results were discarded rather than counted as independent evidence. Seventeen unique official source URLs were read; one additional Bologna URL returned `CRAWL_NOT_FOUND` and was excluded.

## Decisions that follow from the evidence

1. **Prepare for fully funded research as a parallel pathway.** Research admission, scholarship, employer sponsorship, medical registration and visa are separate decisions. None of these programmes automatically transfers a foreign specialist into clinical training or licenses medical practice.
2. **First prove the research component of the existing degree.** A professional MD or medical specialist title cannot be assigned an Australian honours level or Italian second-cycle equivalence by this system. Obtain the award, transcripts with the institution's grading scale, thesis scope/mark, authorship roles and any genuine research outputs. Ask the admitting university for an equivalence determination. Finishing specialist training or passing a board may strengthen a relevant record but is not a universal PhD prerequisite and cannot substitute for a required degree or research component.
3. **Target topic fit rather than collecting unrelated certificates.** Clinical epidemiology, cardiorenal outcomes, public health and health-data projects can be plausible bridges from internal medicine if supported by evidence. A small supervised study with ethics approval where required, a clean analysis and credible referee is stronger preparation than an unrelated paid certificate. This is a reasoned recommendation based on published selection criteria, not an institution's promise of acceptance.
4. **Choose a funding package, not only a stipend title.** Verify tuition, mandatory fees, health insurance, stipend duration, taxation/social contributions, family costs, travel and rent. A fee-only award is not living support; a living stipend without international tuition support may leave a substantial gap. Compare net budgets in the actual city, not nominal currency amounts.
5. **Schedule against availability.** If clinical training completion or release is uncertain, prepare the research file now but choose an enrolment date that is possible. Do not claim an earlier exit is feasible. Board success and non-award can both leave research options open where the published entry degree requirements are met; the university decides equivalence.

## Specific preparation actions and expected benefit

These are planning recommendations, not guaranteed selection criteria beyond the official requirements above.

- **First2weeks:** build a verified research inventory: degree/transcript, thesis abstract and grading, research method, personal contribution, verified bibliography, presentations and two potential referees. Output: factual researchCV and evidence map. Benefit: resolves the biggest eligibility uncertainty before paying fees or selecting a deadline.
- **Next4–8weeks, depending on real workload:** complete a focused research-methods/statistics learning plan and produce one reproducible analysis on appropriately licensed public or synthetic data. Suggested methods: cohort design, confounding, regression and missing-data handling using R/Python. No named course is claimed accepted by a university here. Benefit: concrete proof of capability for research-project fit; it does not replace a degree requirement.
- **Next8–16weeks, when institutionally authorised:** work with a local supervisor on a clinically relevant audit or study, obtain ethics/quality-approval as required and retain a documented role. Use an abstract/poster or manuscript only when real data, permissions and authorship justify it. Benefit: evidence of research potential and a stronger referee; publication timing cannot be guaranteed.
- **Language:** book a recognised test only after choosing the application standard and practical test location. Self-reported B2 is not documentary ELP. Plan for the strictest relevant university/visa/clinical rule, while recognising these standards differ; do not apply Ahpra scores as universal PhD scores.
- **Before any submission:** obtain written supervisor/programme responses on MD equivalence, topic fit, start availability, complete funding and host/country restrictions. Draft all outreach for owner review; this research did not send messages or applications.

## Source quotations supporting key distinctions

- Sydney: “To be eligible to pursue postgraduate research study, you'll need previous research experience and a high academic record.” Application instructions require the scholarship form in addition to course admission.
- Melbourne: selection is “based on their academic results for prior study and their research potential”; specialist medical qualifications “may be taken into consideration.”
- Monash: “There are a number of tuition scholarships available for our international students that can be packaged with this stipend.” FITS: “Overseas Student Health Cover: Not covered by this scholarship.”
- Bologna: “international candidates are eligible for all the scholarships offered”; restricted-topic positions require “a specific assessment by the Committee.”
- Padua42: a foreign qualification must “qualify the holder for admission to a PhD programme within the education system/country of award” and suitability is assessed by the Selection Committee.
- MSCA: researchers “must not have a doctoral degree at the date of their recruitment” and “can be of any nationality.”
- DAAD medicine: “specialist medical training in Germany and any other project that requires the scholarship-holder to work as a doctor ... unfortunately cannot be funded.”

## Boundaries for the dashboard

Always show reviewdate/source and deadline status. Expired records stay clearly `closed`; unknown dates stay unknown. Do not rank by invented acceptance percentages. Show data gaps as unresolved, and distinguish an analyst's recommendation from a host's mandatory requirement. Personal grades, degree dates, language results and documents stay in the private owner record. Research programme permissions and the owner's deliberate sharing remain separate from shared public-source discovery.
