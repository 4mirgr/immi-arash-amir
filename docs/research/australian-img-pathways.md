# Australian IMG pathway evidence

Retrieved 2026-11-03. This is a first evidence register, not an exhaustive state-by-state review or an individual eligibility decision. Registration, recruitment, Medicare eligibility and visa sponsorship are separate decisions. Recheck source revisions before acting.

## Arash: GP employment and PESCI

- AMC Standard Pathway requires CAT MCQ **and** Clinical Examination or accredited workplace-based assessment for an AMC certificate. MCQ passage alone is neither the complete certificate nor registration. Source: [AMC Standard Pathway](https://www.amc.org.au/pathways/standard-pathway/) (full page retrieved).
- [Medical Board PESCI](https://www.medicalboard.gov.au/Registration/International-Medical-Graduates/pesci) (full page retrieved; page says reviewed 10/09/2026): structured clinical interview using scenarios, assessing suitability for a **specific position**, including available supervision, orientation and support. Its report cannot usually transfer to another position; limited exceptions apply. It is not a multiple-choice exam. Specialist pathway applicants are assessed by their college and do not need PESCI on that pathway.
- Standard/Competent Authority applicants seeking provisional/limited registration in general practice need PESCI. Normally arrange before registration application; the Board describes a different order for Standard Pathway limited registration for Area of Need when GP experience has not yet been assessed. That category requires at least three years FTE GP/primary-care experience. Do not book PESCI for that category before RACGP/Board confirms sufficient experience.
- The page lists AMC-accredited PESCI providers ACRRM, RACGP and Institute of Medical Education. Provider availability, fees, interview timing and exact format must be checked with the selected provider; no universal station count or timer is asserted here.
- [Medical Board supervision](https://www.medicalboard.gov.au/Registration/International-Medical-Graduates/Supervision) (official search excerpt): all IMGs with limited or provisional registration must be supervised. A clinic advertising work does not establish its ability to provide an approvable supervision arrangement.

For every potential position collect: dated original vacancy, employing entity, exact address, current vacancy status, registration category accepted, supervisor availability and proposed supervision level, PESCI support, written visa sponsorship terms, remuneration/contract terms and source. Unstated information stays unknown. Uploaded clinic contacts are leads, not confirmed vacancies or offers. Never turn a match score into a probability of acceptance.

## Amir: internal medicine specialist and training alternatives

[Full RACP Standard Specialist Assessment page](https://www.racp.edu.au/overseas-trained-physicians/international-medical-graduates/standard-specialist-assessment-pathway) retrieved:

- Requires a verified international specialist qualification with minimum three years of specialist training in an RACP specialty. Shorter training relative to Australian training may need significant independent consultant experience; assessment is individual.
- Primary and specialist qualifications require primary-source verification. Training, assessments, recent clinical practice, consultant experience and CPD are compared. Outcomes: substantially comparable, partially comparable or not comparable. Up to 12 months FTE peer review for substantial comparability; up to 24 months FTE supervised practice for partial comparability. The actual decision sets the applicant's requirements.
- Position and supervision must be approved by RACP before work under that assessment starts. A supervisor's invitation alone does not grant specialist registration or substitute for college/Board approval.
- The current page lists specific qualifications from several countries for its Accelerated Specialist Pathway. Do not infer that an Iranian qualification qualifies for an accelerated or Medical Board expedited pathway without checking the current accepted qualification list; these are distinct pathways.
- Iranian board status alone cannot determine comparability. Still needed: exact final specialist award and awarding authority, completion certificate, curriculum and rotations, duration/FTE, internship, licensure and registration history, consultant experience, procedure logbook without patient identifiers, CPD, English evidence and verification status. Model “board obtained” and “not obtained” separately; ask RACP to confirm whether the precise award meets eligibility. Do not promise eligibility without board or assume board guarantees it.

[Apply for Physician Training](https://www.racp.edu.au/become-a-physician/apply-for-physician-training) and [Recognition of Prior Learning](https://www.racp.edu.au/trainees/recognition-of-prior-learning) (full pages retrieved): Basic Training requires general medical registration and employment at an accredited hospital, with the stated degree requirements. IMGs have the same training eligibility requirements as local graduates. Previous training can be considered through formal RPL; the application page says apply within six months of starting the program. Curriculum/stage rules differ. An employer's registrar title or supervisor recommendation does not establish college-accredited training credit, entry at year two or automatic exemption from exams. Track employment grade, accredited training enrolment and RPL decision separately. Hospital non-training posts, formal training, specialist assessment and short-term specialist training must be separate opportunity types.

## Geography and workforce classifications

Official search excerpts retrieved from Department of Health pages:

- [DPA](https://www.health.gov.au/topics/rural-health-workforce/classifications/dpa): GP workforce access classification relevant to IMG Medicare access.
- [DWS](https://www.health.gov.au/topics/rural-health-workforce/classifications/dws): specialist workforce shortage classification.
- [Area of Need](https://www.health.gov.au/topics/rural-health-workforce/classifications/aon): a separate designation relevant to need and limited-registration employment.
- [Modified Monash Model](https://www.health.gov.au/topics/rural-health-workforce/classifications/mmm): geographic rurality/remoteness classification, distinct from DPA, DWS, supervision level or registration category. MM1–MM7 are not a grading of applicant eligibility.

DPA full-page retrieval was refused by Firecrawl (unsupported site); direct HTTPS returned 503 for DPA and MMM. Therefore this first pass does not certify a clinic's current DPA/MMM status. Verify its exact location against the current official workforce locator before filtering it as eligible. Store each classification with source, effective/retrieved date and verification status.
