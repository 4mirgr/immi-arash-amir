# Public-page reading and extractive evaluation

`analyzePublicPage(record, {profileId='amir', country='IT'})` in `cloud/public-page-analysis.mjs` is a pure, deterministic evaluator of an **actually read** public page. The caller provides `{url,title,text,status:'read'|'read_partial',readAt,institutionIds}` from the content reader. A discovered link, blocked response, missing read timestamp, non-HTTPS source or insufficient text returns `null`. A genuinely retrieved partial PDF/text is analyzable only with `partialReading:true`, preserved reader limitations and `sourceReadStatus`; it is never represented as fully read. Italy is Amir-only; invalid scopes throw. No profile/CV data, credentials, private mail, AI model or network is read.

The successful contract contains `version`, `profileId`, `country`, `category` (`call`, `admission`, `funding`, `career`, `development`, `guidance`, `navigation`, `unrelated`), `topics`, `healthRelevance`, `partialReading`, `sourceReadStatus`, `textEvaluatedCharacters`, `omittedLongPassages`, extractive `summary`, and arrays `requirements`, `funding`, `deadlines`, `actions` of `{text}`. It also returns `archiveEvidence`, `uncertainty`, `sourceUrl`, `readAt`, `institutionIds`, `eligibility:'not_assessed'`, `currentCallStatus:'not_verified'`, `reviewStatus:'machine_extracted_requires_review'` and `limitations`.

Every summary sentence and excerpt comes from retrieved text. Negation, conditions and advertised amounts/deadlines remain verbatim. There are no inferred acceptance percentages, qualification checks, translated promises, parsed deadline cutoffs, guaranteed funding or automatic actions. `category:'call'` can describe a call index or archive; it is not evidence of an individual open vacancy. Academic admission is not clinical registration. AU job/career URLs with explicit health content, and strong clinical recruitment titles, retain the career category even when the description mentions a funded salary, PhD requirement or medical-college fellowship. RACGP/ACRRM fellowship qualifications are not funding evidence by themselves. A generic doctoral/funding page can be useful while `healthRelevance:'not_stated'` remains explicit.

The evaluator reads at most 50,000 text characters and keeps at most six 700-character excerpts in each evidence field. Longer passages are omitted with an explicit count and limitation instead of clipping off a late negation or condition. It suppresses obvious cookie/privacy boilerplate and prompt-injection instructions from extracted recommendations. This does not confer trust on arbitrary page instructions: the module never executes or sends anything. PDF annexes and further links remain independent unread documents until the reader retrieves them separately.

UI and importers must retain source/read date, review status and limits beside extracted findings. Do not turn all classified pages into `opportunity_editions`, silently change CV achievements, activate sending or announce eligibility from this output. A qualified content review and the full original call remain necessary for personal conclusions.

Verification: `node --test tests/cloud_public_page_analysis.mjs` covers unread/blocked input, owner scope, Italian clinical research calls, negation, conditional funding, archive evidence, admission/registration separation, boilerplate, prompt injection, AU IMG/PESCI wording and bounds. No external operation is performed.
